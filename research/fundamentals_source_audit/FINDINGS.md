# Fundamentals source audit, 11 November 2026

This audit investigates the source change around the March 2025 quarter and the 15-position CSM Value run saved under `csm_value/output`. The raw fundamentals, trading strategy, and exit-day weighting were not changed. This is an investigation, not a controlled re-run with corrected data.

## Confirmed findings

1. **Bank results are missing after December 2024.** HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK and SBIN have later public NSE filings, but their local financial results stop at the December 2024 quarter. `update_integrated_fundamentals.py` rejects filings without `revenue_from_operations`, whereas the banking taxonomy uses `InterestEarned`. Running the updater's parser against these three original filings reproduced the rejection. The 140-day freshness rule eventually makes stale old results unavailable. The complete bank-related impact has not been measured.

2. **Filing revisions replaced original history.** The updater keeps the latest filing for each quarter and basis, so an original filing can be lost for the period before its revision. The strategy correctly avoids using the revision before its own date, but then has no original version to use. The loader also discards filings dated more than 120 days after the reporting period ends. Among main-board financial blocks ending in 2025–26, 416 retained revisions across 278 symbols have lags greater than 120 days. These are filing/basis rows, not necessarily 416 lost trades or 278 entirely unusable companies. For example, PFC's consolidated March 2026 original was filed on 13 May 2026; the retained revision is dated 21 August 2026, 143 days after quarter-end, and the loader drops it. The standalone March result remains available, so PFC is an example of a lost consolidated basis, not necessarily a completely missing company.

3. **There are source-level scale inconsistencies.** NAVNETEDUL's local consolidated March 2026 revenue and profit are ₹4.30 crore and ₹0.39 crore. Its original NSE XBRL contains those same amounts expressed in rupees. The company's own investor presentation reports ₹430 crore revenue and ₹39 crore profit for that same quarter, 100 times larger. The locally stored share capital for the quarter is also 100 times smaller than the following quarter, while face value is unchanged. This can distort both trailing earnings and shares used to compute earnings yield; the impact on trades depends on all eligibility filters. NAVNETEDUL did not appear in the saved 15-position trade log after this filing. The older PFC December 2023 data also has an apparent 100-fold discontinuity, so this issue is not limited to the new collection period. Do not apply an automatic 100-fold correction to every flagged row without verifying each source and its units.

4. **The source transition did not erase the checked old data.** Comparing 1,964 main-board backup files with the merged files found no changed or missing values across 67,840 pre-2025 quarterly/basis blocks for five fields: revenue, net profit, basic EPS, paid-up equity capital, and face value. This does not verify other fields, every SME file, or whether the historical data was correct before merging.

5. **No broad collapse in usable fundamentals is evident.** Median monthly counts of companies with a fresh four-quarter earnings sum were 1,700 in 2024, 1,777 in 2025, and 1,765 in 2026 through the end of the saved run. This uses the same loader and freshness setting and the overlap with OHLCV and available NSE price symbols. It is not a count of stocks passing all the strategy's filters, and it does not disprove errors in individual companies.

## Performance context

The saved 15-position run returned -14.14% in calendar 2025 and -9.26% in 2026 through 4 November; through 28 August 2026 it returned -2.47%. These figures use the existing portfolio accounting including the intentionally zero exit-day weight.

Reported exceptional gains are another possible contributor, separate from incorrect data. For DEVIT, the NSE September 2025 quarter reports ₹93.5464 crore in exceptional items before tax and ₹71.8766 crore net profit; the saved strategy entered on 1 December 2025 and the trade log records -18.15%. These are evidence that a large exceptional item and a losing trade coincided, not a calculation of its contribution to portfolio returns. Other income is not necessarily one-time income, and a filter would require separate testing.

To measure performance impact, reconstruct an independently verified point-in-time dataset with original and revised versions, restore banking filings, resolve individual unit discrepancies against reliable contemporaneous disclosures, and repeat the same 15-position backtest with only the input dataset changed. Compare holdings, signals, trades and annual returns; retain all existing execution and exit-day conventions. Separately test any earnings-quality rule using the established in-sample/out-of-sample protocol.
