## 2. How the cut was made

**Common chassis.** Every stock idea was run as a monthly top-15 equal-weight book, 0.3% one-way costs, then the *same* overlay: SMA200 market gate (50% exposure in bear), 20% volatility target (6-month trailing book vol, lagged one month), and cost on exposure changes. Control = the Elendel signal (`a3_rs_high` + `q5_126`) run through that identical chassis. This removes the "stripped book vs live book" apples-to-oranges problem within the comparison, though not versus your live engine.

**"Live-like bucket"** = equal-weight blend of four books on that chassis (Elendel, Zenith, csm_absolute score, residual momentum). It is a proxy for your 5–6 live momentum strategies, not the real thing; the true marginal value of any idea will differ.

**Rubric** (written after the exploratory study and before the stress tests were run; it is a decision rule, not a result). BUILD requires all of:

1. **Adds to the bucket:** ΔSharpe ≥ +0.05 with block-bootstrap P(Δ>0) ≥ 0.95 at a 20% weight — *or* ΔMax DD ≥ 3 points with ΔSharpe ≥ 0 (diversifier).
2. **Holds since 2015:** standalone 2015+ Sharpe ≥ the control's, *or* correlation to the bucket < 0.6.
3. **Survives 2.5× costs:** Sharpe at 0.75% one-way ≥ the control's Sharpe at 0.30%.
4. **Tradable:** a position of AUM/15 (or /20) is within 5% of average daily traded value for at least the median pick at ₹10 cr.
5. **Survives the live chassis:** still beats the control when the SMA200 universe filter used by `csm_elendel_strategy.py` is applied (where that chassis is intended).

## 5. Build specifications, evidence and kill criteria

Kill criteria are what the full event-driven engine (your stops, Crash Guard, hysteresis, real costs) must show before capital is committed. They are set deliberately below the backtest numbers so that a pass is meaningful but not a coin flip.

### Build 1 — Gold sleeve  (implemented in `gold_sleeve/`)

* **Spec:** `GoldSleevePV` — exposure = persistence × volatility scale, decided at month-end, traded next close: persistence = share of the last 126 days gold closed above its 50-day SMA; volatility scale = min(1, 12% / 63-day annualised vol); remainder in cash/liquid fund. Instrument GOLDBEES (₹238 cr median daily value). `mode="buy_hold"` is built in for A/B. Parity-tested against the research series (exact), gap-safe, with a stale-data guard for live use.
* **Why this logic:** gold timing has **no demonstrable return edge** here — the best time-series signal (12-month momentum) has a t-stat of 1.1 and classic TSMOM/SMA rules did not beat buy-and-hold as a sleeve. The persistence × volatility rule is chosen for what a sleeve is for: at an equal risk budget to a 10% buy-and-hold sleeve (≈19% weight) the blended max drawdown was shallower in 81/81 neighbouring parameter sets (+1.4 to +3.5 pts) with unchanged Sharpe, and the real exposure path beat 86–90% of time-shifted placebo paths on drawdown.
* **Evidence (usable months only, gap excluded):** standalone sleeve CAGR 11.5%, vol 8.4%, Sharpe 0.64, max DD -6.9%; since 2015: CAGR 10.7%, Sharpe 0.59. Buy-and-hold gold on the same months: CAGR 15.8%, max DD -17.3%. Buy-and-hold gold at 20% of the bucket: ΔSharpe +0.16 (P=1.00), max DD -26.7% → -17.8%; correlation with the bucket **-0.23**.
* **Expectation:** planning CAGR 8%–9% and max DD -9% to -10% standalone (a floor: the unobserved 2011–14 years and single-day gold gaps like January 2026's −10% are not in the drawdown). It gives up about 4–5 CAGR points versus holding gold (it averages ~52% exposure) in exchange for a drawdown about 10 points shallower. Do not expect a return boost; 2008–2026 was a strong INR gold period.
* **Risk:** monthly decisions only — no intra-month stop; gold can gap (−10% in one day on 2026-01-30). The rule de-risked from 96% (Aug 2025) to ~24% (Jul 2026) as volatility rose. Taxes on frequent rebalancing are not modelled.
* **Kill/Review:** drop the timing logic and hold gold statically (or revisit sizing) if (i) the live sleeve's drawdown is not clearly shallower than buy-and-hold over a full gold drawdown, or (ii) the rolling 3-year correlation of gold with the equity bucket rises above +0.3.

### Build 2 — I1 Tight-range trend

* **Spec:** copy `csm_elendel_strategy.py`; replace the factor pair with `tight_close_15` (= −std(close, 15d) / mean(close, 15d)) and `q5_126`, z-scored and summed; set `universe_top_n_min=301`, `universe_top_n_max=1000`; keep SMA200 filter, absolute-momentum gate, top-15 with hysteresis/swap-gap, inverse-vol sizing, Crash Guard. In the proxy the overlay was an SMA200 market gate + 20% vol target.
* **Evidence (live-style chassis, with SMA200 universe filter):** full-sample CAGR 33.7%, Sharpe 1.20, max DD -30.8%; since 2015 CAGR 22.3%, Sharpe 0.82, max DD -30.8% versus the control's 2015+ Sharpe 0.74. ΔSharpe vs control +0.28 full / +0.07 since 2015. Adds +0.11 Sharpe to the bucket (P=1.00) at a 20% weight for the unfiltered version, and +0.09 at a 15% weight for the live-style version (blend table, §1). Mid/small IC is strong (ICIR 0.92, t 11) and the tightness leg is nearly uncorrelated with Elendel (corr ≈ 0). **But most of the full-sample lead is from 2004–2014:** since 2015 the edge over the Elendel control is only about +1.2 CAGR points and +0.10 Sharpe (unfiltered chassis).
* **Expectation:** plan for **Sharpe 0.41–0.65, CAGR 12%–17%, max DD -39% to -46%** — i.e. mid-teens, not the 33% full-sample backtest (which is dominated by 2004–2014). Cost-robust: Sharpe 1.21 / 1.09 / 0.93 / 0.78 at 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0% one-way.
* **Risks:** turnover ≈ 0.65 of the book per month in the proxy (hysteresis should cut it); mid/small capacity ≈ ₹10 cr at ≤5% of ADV per position (about half of Elendel's); tight closes can reflect thin trading; correlation with Elendel book 0.81–0.88, so it is a *better* momentum book, not a different return stream.
* **Kill criteria (full engine):** 2015+ Sharpe ≥ 0.55 **and** ≥ the same-engine Elendel + 0.05; max DD ≤ 35%; monthly turnover ≤ 0.8. Ablation: if `q5_126` alone on the same universe gives ≥ the same Sharpe, drop the tightness leg.

### Build 3 (conditional) — I6 Breakout v3

* **Spec:** liquid-1000 universe; signal = close above prior 252-day closing high and no such breakout in the previous 10 days; require top-quartile 6-month (lag 1-month) return rank and the Stage-2 template (C>SMA50>SMA150>SMA200, SMA200 rising, within 25% of 52w high, >30% above 52w low); **no volume rule**; enter next close; 20 slots, each 1/20 of equity; rank by RS when more signals than slots; exit next close after a close ≤ entry −10% or ≤ peak −20%; no new entries while the equal-weight index is below its SMA200.
* **Evidence (prototype, 0.3% one-way, idle cash 6%):** CAGR 26.9%, Sharpe 0.95, max DD -30.2%; since 2015 CAGR 21.8%, Sharpe 0.75; 783 trades, win rate 39%, average win 64% vs average loss -11% (payoff 5.8×). Across the 12 pre-set exit/gate variants of the two RS-gated signals, Sharpe ranges 0.74–1.12; none were tuned. At 1.0% one-way costs Sharpe is 0.83 (2015+ 0.60).
* **Expectation:** plan for **Sharpe 0.37–0.59, CAGR 12%–17%, max DD -38% to -45%** and a **~60% losing-trade rate** — you must be able to tolerate that. Adds +0.07 Sharpe to the bucket (P=0.997); correlation with the bucket 0.79.
* **Risks:** the prototype ignores circuit-limit fills, gap-through-stop and per-name ADV limits (33% of entries exceed 5% of ADV at ₹10 cr); event-study evidence shows the edge is momentum/RS in disguise, so correlation to your momentum books is high; ungated 52w-high breakouts did about as well in the prototype once slots are ranked by RS, so the RS **ranking** (not the gate) is what carries it.
* **Kill criteria (HR/VCP chassis):** 2015+ Sharpe ≥ 0.55; win rate 33–45% and payoff ≥ 4×; Sharpe range across the three stop settings (8/15, 10/20, 12/25) ≤ 0.4 (else fragile).

### Optional — I3 Low-risk sleeve

* **Spec:** factors `lowvol_126` (−std of daily returns, 126d) + `low_ulcer_252` (−RMS drawdown from trailing 252d peak), top-250 liquidity universe, top-15, **no SMA200 universe filter** and no absolute-momentum gate (neither was in the tested book); keep the market gate/Crash Guard.
* **Evidence:** full-sample CAGR 17.8%, Sharpe 0.81, max DD -32.0%; since 2015 CAGR 12.5%, Sharpe 0.56, max DD -17.2%. With the live SMA200 universe filter it falls to a 2015+ Sharpe of 0.28 (CAGR 8.9%). In the bucket at 20%: ΔSharpe +0.05 (P=0.97), max DD −3.5 pts, correlation 0.51.
* **Expectation:** plan for Sharpe 0.28–0.45, CAGR 9%–11%. It is a risk-reduction sleeve; **do not expect it to earn its keep as a return source**. Capacity is the best of the four (≈ ₹95 cr).
* **Kill criteria:** 2015+ Sharpe ≥ 0.4 in the full engine and correlation to the bucket ≤ 0.65; otherwise drop (gold already provides most of the diversification).

## 6. How to read the "planning expectation"

The backtests are optimistic in ways that cannot all be measured, so each build idea carries an explicit haircut. **This is judgment, not a statistical estimate.**

* **Sharpe:** 50%–80% of the idea's **2015+** Sharpe (the more recent and less-flattering period; the same idea earned much more in 2004–2014).
* **CAGR:** 6% (cash) + Sharpe × 2015+ volatility − volatility²/2.
* **Max drawdown:** the full-sample worst drawdown × 1.25–1.5 (the sample contains 2008 and 2020 but not every possible path).
* **Portfolio improvement:** 50%–80% of the measured 2015+ improvement.
* **Why the haircut:** (i) survivorship — only ~2% of symbols are delisted (removing the known delisted names changed results by <0.02 Sharpe, so the bias comes from names missing entirely and cannot be measured here); (ii) the factor pairs were chosen after seeing single-factor results; (iii) 2015+ decay — price-based IC was 30–40% lower after 2014; (iv) the proxy ignores execution frictions (circuit limits, partial fills).

## 9. Caveats (what these numbers cannot tell you)

* **Proxy, not your engine.** The "bucket" and the control are simplified books on a shared chassis. Your live strategies have their own stops, hysteresis and regime logic, so absolute levels and marginal gains will differ; only the *ranking* of ideas should be trusted at this stage.
* **Survivorship** is unquantified and flatters every long-only price signal (low-risk and smooth-trend screens most).
* **Selection effects:** the four builds were chosen from ~20 ideas, the pairs were formed after single-factor results, and the blend weights (10/15/10/10) were fixed by hand, not optimised. The bootstrap intervals describe sampling noise in the history, not this selection risk.
* **Regime dependence:** gold's sample was favourable (CAGR 23.1% before 2015, 14.1% since). Returns faded after 2014 for the equity ideas: tight-range trend CAGR 46% → 23%, breakout prototype 32% → 22%, Elendel control 24% → 22%.
* **Breakout prototype** assumes fills at the close after the signal/stop with no circuit or liquidity effects; real slippage on stop exits in small caps can be materially worse.
* **Capacity** is computed from 2022–2026 traded values; it was far lower before and can shrink in a market downturn.
